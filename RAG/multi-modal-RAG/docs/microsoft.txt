MICROSOFT CORPORATION
====================

Microsoft Corporation is an American multinational technology conglomerate headquartered in Redmond, Washington. Founded in 1975, the company became influential in the rise of personal computers through software like Windows, and has since expanded to Internet services, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, video gaming, and other fields. Often described as a Big Tech company, Microsoft is the largest software company by revenue, one of the most valuable public companies, and one of the most valuable brands globally.

COMPANY INFORMATION:

- Founders: Bill Gates, Paul Allen
- Headquarters: Redmond, Washington, U.S.
- Chairman & CEO: Satya Nadella
- Number of Employees: 228,000 (2025)
- Revenue: US$281.7 billion (2025)
- Net Income: US$101.8 billion (2025)
- Traded as: NASDAQ: MSFT

HISTORY AND FOUNDING:
Childhood friends Bill Gates and Paul Allen sought to make a business using their skills in computer programming. In 1972, they founded Traf-O-Data, which sold a rudimentary computer to track and analyze automobile traffic data.

In January 1975, the January issue of Popular Electronics featured the Altair 8800 microcomputer, which inspired Allen to suggest that they could program a BASIC interpreter for the device. Gates called MITS and claimed he had a working interpreter. MITS requested a demonstration, and it worked flawlessly when they demonstrated it in March 1975 in Albuquerque, New Mexico.

Gates and Allen established Microsoft on April 4, 1975, with Gates as CEO, and Allen suggested the name "Micro-Soft", short for micro-computer software.

MAJOR MILESTONES:
- August 1977: First international office in Japan (ASCII Microsoft)
- January 1979: Moved headquarters to Bellevue, Washington
- November 1980: IBM contracted Microsoft to provide OS for IBM PC
- 1981: IBM PC released with MS-DOS
- November 20, 1985: Released Windows 1.0
- March 13, 1986: IPO on NASDAQ, making four billionaires and 12,000 millionaires
- May 1990: Introduced Microsoft Office
- January 13, 2000: Bill Gates handed CEO position to Steve Ballmer
- October 25, 2001: Released Windows XP
- November 2005: Released Xbox 360
- January 2007: Released Windows Vista
- October 27, 2008: Launched Azure cloud platform
- October 22, 2009: First Microsoft Store opened; Windows 7 released
- October 2010: Released Windows Phone
- May 2012: Acquired Skype Technologies
- June 2012: Released Surface (first in-house PC line)
- 2014: Satya Nadella became CEO, shifting focus to cloud computing
- December 2014: Released Xbox One
- April 2, 2019: Became third U.S. company valued over $1 trillion
- 2023: Acquired Activision Blizzard for $68.7 billion

RECENT STRATEGIC INITIATIVES:

FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE:
Microsoft became the third U.S. company to reach $1 trillion valuation in April 2019. In 2024-2025, it has consistently traded places with Apple and Nvidia as the world's most valuable company. Revenue exceeded $281 billion in 2025, with net income of $101.8 billion, reflecting strong growth in cloud services and AI products.

CONTROVERSIES:
- Long history of antitrust litigation
- Criticized for monopolistic practices
- Ease of use, robustness, and security issues with software
- Recent criticism for role in providing services to Israel during Gaza war
- Monopoly concerns in operating systems and office software

EMPLOYEE BASE:
With 228,000 employees as of 2025, Microsoft has grown significantly. The company employs talent across research labs in Cambridge (England), Beijing, Bengaluru (India), Cambridge (Massachusetts), New York, and Montreal.

FUTURE OUTLOOK:
Microsoft is positioning itself as the AI-first company, heavily investing in AI infrastructure and products. The partnership with OpenAI and development of Copilot across all products indicate a strategic pivot toward AI as the core technology driving future growth.
