TESLA, INC.
===========

Tesla, Inc. (TESS-lə or TEZ-lə) is an American multinational automotive and clean energy company. Headquartered in Austin, Texas, it designs, manufactures, and sells battery electric vehicles (BEVs), stationary battery energy storage devices from home to grid-scale, solar panels and solar shingles, and related products and services.

COMPANY INFORMATION:
- Founded: July 1, 2003 (as Tesla Motors, Inc.)
- Founders: Martin Eberhard, Marc Tarpenning (co-founders)
- Headquarters: Austin, Texas, US
- CEO: Elon Musk
- Number of Employees: 134,785 (2025)
- Revenue: US$94.83 billion (2025)
- Net Income: US$3.79 billion (2025)
- Traded as: NASDAQ: TSLA

HISTORY AND FOUNDING:
The company was incorporated as Tesla Motors, Inc., on July 1, 2003, by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning. The company's name is a tribute to the inventor and electrical engineer Nikola Tesla.

In February 2004, Elon Musk led Tesla's first funding round of $7.5 million and became the company's chairman. In 2008, Musk was named chief executive officer. A lawsuit settlement agreed to in September 2009 allows Eberhard, Tarpenning, Wright, Musk, and Straubel to all call themselves co-founders.

MAJOR MILESTONES:
- 2008: Began production of Roadster sports car
- June 29, 2010: Tesla went public via IPO on NASDAQ
- 2012: Launched Model S sedan
- 2013: Model S topped Norwegian new car sales
- 2015: Launched Model X SUV
- 2016: Unveiled first mass-market vehicle, Model 3
- 2017: Model 3 became world's best-selling electric car
- December 21, 2020: Added to S&P 500 index
- October 2021: Reached $1 trillion market capitalization
- 2020: Launched Model Y crossover
- 2022: Launched Tesla Semi truck
- 2023: Launched Cybertruck
- November 2025: Tesla lost status as world's leading EV manufacturer to BYD

KEY PRODUCTS:
- Roadster (sports car)
- Model S (sedan)
- Model 3 (compact sedan)
- Model X (SUV)
- Model Y (crossover)
- Cybertruck (pickup truck)
- Tesla Semi (truck)
- Powerwall (home battery)
- Powerpack (business battery)
- Megapack (grid-scale battery)
- Solar Panels and Solar Roof

MARKET POSITION:
- Starting in July 2020: world's most valuable automaker
- October 2021 to March 2022: US$1 trillion company
- June 2023: sixth US company to reach $1 trillion valuation
- 2024: Led battery electric vehicle market with 17.6% share
- 2025: Exceeded $1 trillion market cap again (Nov 2024-Feb 2025, May 2025+)
- 2025: Lost leadership position to BYD in global EV sales

GIGAFACTORIES:
- Fremont, California (first Tesla Factory, 2010)
- Gigafactory Shanghai (2019, China's first foreign-owned auto factory)
- Gigafactory Berlin (opened 2022)
- Gigafactory Texas (opened 2022, moved legal headquarters here)
- Gigafactory Mexico (announced 2023, groundbreaking delayed)

KEY LEADERSHIP:
- CEO: Elon Musk
- Chair: Robyn Denholm
- Early Co-founders: Martin Eberhard, Marc Tarpenning, J.B. Straubel

BUSINESS EXPANSION:
- 2016: Acquired SolarCity for $2.6 billion
- 2015: Entered energy storage market with Powerwall and Powerpack
- 2020: Became major bitcoin investor ($1.5 billion)
- 2023: Started shipping Cybertruck

MAJOR ACHIEVEMENTS:
- Model 3 became world's best-selling electric car 2018-2021
- Model Y became best-selling vehicle globally (2023)
- Powerwall and battery storage market leadership
- Vertical integration of manufacturing and technology
- Leading autopilot/autonomous driving capabilities

PRODUCTION AND DELIVERY:
- 2025: Delivered 1.66 million vehicles
- 2025: 46.7 GWh battery energy storage systems
- 1,359 sales, service, and delivery centers globally
- 7,791 Supercharger stations worldwide

CONTROVERSIES AND CRITICISMS:
- Whistleblower retaliation allegations
- Worker rights violations including sexual harassment
- Anti-union activities
- Safety defects leading to dozens of recalls
- Lack of public relations department
- Overpromising on Full Self-Driving capabilities
- Delayed product releases
- Factory working conditions
- Environmental concerns about lithium mining

FINANCIAL STRUGGLES AND SUCCESS:
- Early years: Nearly bankrupt (2008-2009)
- 2009: Received $465 million government loan from DOE
- 2010: Repaid loan with interest ahead of schedule
- 2020: 740% stock price increase during pandemic
- December 2020: Market cap exceeded next 9 largest automakers combined

ELON MUSK'S COMPENSATION:
- November 2025: Tesla shareholders approved $1 trillion pay package for Musk
- To be received over 10 years if specific performance goals are met

COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE:
- 2025: Lost world's largest BEV manufacturer title to Chinese competitor BYD
- January 28, 2026: Musk stated Tesla would discontinue Model S and Model X in Q2 2026 to focus on Optimus robot manufacturing

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS:
- Continued expansion in battery technology
- Development of autonomous driving technology
- Entry into robotics market (Tesla Bot/Optimus)
- Focus on manufacturing efficiency and cost reduction
- International expansion and localization strategies
- Energy storage business growth
